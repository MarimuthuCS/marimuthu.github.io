04 Case in point

A two-minute report, now under a second.

A multi-property hotel report took about two minutes to load. I rewrote the query, added indexing and normalized the data behind it. Same report, about 150 times faster.

Before
120 s
After
0.8 s
SELECT p.name, COUNT(c.id) AS done
FROM checklists c
JOIN properties p ON p.id = c.property_id
GROUP BY p.id; -- indexed · rewritten
